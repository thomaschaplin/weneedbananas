🍌 The shopping list you share with three words.

No app. No signup. No password. Start a list, get three random words like lemon.fork.bulb, and send them to whoever you shop with. Anyone with the words is on the list.

What it does:
🗺️ Sorts itself into aisle order, so you get round the shop in one lap
🧮 Understands quantities: add 6 eggs twice and you get 12 eggs, not two lines of eggs
🔒 Syncs phone to phone, encrypted. There's no server storing your list
🛒 Shop mode: big one-thumb ticks and your screen stays awake
👀 Shows everyone when someone's at the shop, so you can add the thing you just remembered
📴 Works offline

0 accounts. 0 servers. 0 ads. Free.
